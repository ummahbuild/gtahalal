# Dunya — Founder Perspectives: Industry Story Audit and Playable Mission Design

**Status:** audit and original narrative expansion only; no claim of playable implementation or religious approval. This document uses the inspected *Perspectives of Muslim Founders* manuscript (Parts III, IV, V, VIII) and the inspected ummah.build Weekly Content Calendar as creative frameworks, not as a license to copy text or represent fictional events as real corporate misconduct.

## Source-derived principles versus new design

**From manuscript:** (1) information, urgency and exit asymmetries; (2) character→product→company→institution→civilization; (3) governance and contract incentives; (4) the year-five maintenance/waqf question; (5) the illustrative fintech founder whose strongest retention can conceal customer dependency; (6) a healthcare product that needs clinician-led religious literacy. These are ideas, not automatically validated religious rulings.

**From ummah.build calendar:** pain-point hook, builder spotlight, thought leadership, open-source resource, project showcase and community story. Adapt these as **optional learning-card types** and post-mission real-world actions, never an addictive daily login calendar.

**New Dunya design:** fictional NPCs, level names, decisions, mechanics, branches and simulation outcomes below. No literal real corporate allegations or direct adaptation of unpublished manuscript characters without rights review.

## Audit finding

The existing industry parody bible covers 11 corporate categories but repeats nearly identical generic choices and dialogue across chapters. The expansion packs add finance, humanitarian, environment, gambling harm and nightlife, yet many are still prose-only. Missing: unique founder conflicts, actual player verbs, asymmetric stakeholder power, operational metrics, consequence persistence, counterarguments, repairs and review evidence. The matrix below supplies **distinct mission briefs**, not release-ready scripts.

**Fictional counterpart:** Looply. **Opening conflict:** Creators benefit from reach but the growth team rewards repeated compulsive sessions.

**Three branching choices:** Publish session-health metrics; retain high-engagement ranking with safeguards; offer chronological and portable feeds.

**Player action:** Player designs a ranking-policy panel and simulates creator migration. **World state:** Creator income, user autonomy, ad revenue, trust. **Repair path:** The board can demand a revised revenue plan; users can reverse a bad migration.

## Cross-vertical long-form arc — The Measure of Success

**Episode A — The Metric:** a founder's dashboard shows growth; a community member reveals a hidden cost. Player must inspect disaggregated fictional outcomes rather than one aggregate score.

**Episode B — The Investor:** capital arrives with growth and governance terms; the player compares ownership, downside and accountability.

**Episode C — The Institution:** the company becomes essential to food, healthcare, water or education access; outages expose the cost of dependence.

**Episode D — The Counterargument:** an incumbent employee shows what the original system did well, forcing the player to preserve benefits while repairing harms.

**Episode E — Year Five:** maintenance, succession, legal compliance and community governance decide whether the alternative survives. Endings include reform, cooperation, independent alternative, transparent failure and responsible wind-down.

## Story quality audit rubric (0–2 each)

- **Specificity:** original character, setting and tension rather than generic corporate villain.
- **Agency:** affected community sets priorities; protagonist does not rescue passive NPCs.
- **Counterargument:** incumbent's legitimate benefit and constraints are represented.
- **Player verb:** observable interactive action, not just reading a paragraph.
- **Branching:** three materially distinct peaceful choices, plus repair.
- **Economics:** cost, cash runway, labor, operational burden and failure modes.
- **Evidence:** verified facts distinguished from claims and fictional composites.
- **Faith discipline:** no automatic fiqh verdict, piety metric or unlicensed translation.
- **Continuity:** persistent state changes another mission or chapter.
- **Real-world-first:** natural stopping point and optional private offline action.

**Passing threshold:** ≥16/20 with no zero on agency, evidence or faith discipline. This is an editorial planning rubric, not a religious approval score.

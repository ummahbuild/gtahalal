# DUNYA activity concept art

These five independent frames establish the visual target for planned activities. Each depicts one player decision. The attached references informed framing, light and interface hierarchy. All five illustrations were generated for this brief. Third-party artwork, game logos and audio are outside this asset set. License and religious-content review are pending before production use.

## Visual requirements

Frame each scene in landscape at approximately 16:9. Light the environment from a believable source. Show people at eye level for dialogue, move close enough to read hand movements for practice, and pull back to show both paths at a navigation decision. Keep the subject, hands and destination clear of overlays.

Place an original heavy white DUNYA wordmark and gold rule above a compact translucent charcoal objective card. Use condensed white labels and reserve gold for focus and destination markers. Give each action a text label. Use the lower-left minimap only during navigation. Keep dialogue choices equally legible, including the exit. Preserve the concept-art label in promotional crops. Check labels against the brightest background at phone size. Implement controls as accessible interface elements separate from illustrations.

## Activity frames

| Frame | Player action and acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| [Wudu Warrior](wudu-warrior.webp) | At the tiled wash station the hands and water stream remain visible. Replay repeats the current step. Next advances at the learner’s pace. Sequence and school-specific guidance require review. |
| [Mosque Maze](mosque-maze.webp) | At a fork the courtyard is recognizable through the left arch. The map must place the waypoint on that same branch. |
| [Tasbih](tasbih.webp) | The beads and manual count share the frame. Add one increments once. Undo reverses the last increment. Keep counts private and noncompetitive. |
| [Qur’an Recitation](quran-recitation.webp) | A quiet listening station offers playback, passage replay and private practice. Use reviewed, licensed recordings and verified text before displaying a passage. |
| [Street Dawah](street-dawah.webp) | At the bookstall the player may ask permission or let the passerby continue. A decline ends the exchange without penalty. |

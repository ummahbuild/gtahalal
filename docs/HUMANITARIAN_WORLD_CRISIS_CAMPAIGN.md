# Dunya — Humanitarian World: Witness, Protect, Rebuild
Status: **draft narrative and educational requirements, not live humanitarian operations or verified current crisis coverage**. The world includes Muslim-majority and Muslim-minority communities, with universal civilian dignity and equal protection regardless of religion. Real events require independent dated sourcing and locally led editorial review; fictional characters must not be confused with real survivors.

## Story premise
After Bayhaven's pantry and financial cooperative begin to stabilize, Fatimah receives an invitation from a fictional diaspora-led humanitarian design consortium. Ahmed joins as a community logistics volunteer. The protagonists are **supporting characters**, not saviors. Local professionals, refugees, women, disabled people, educators and community organizations set priorities. The player's task is to understand the crisis, identify the responsible institutions and constraints without inventing accusations, and build useful civilian systems.

## World layers
1. **Crisis atlas (real-world educational layer):** source/date, jurisdiction, affected communities, displacement, food, health, education, shelter, WASH, access constraints, funding and uncertainties. A factual card is never a gameplay objective or claim of live safety.
2. **Fictional simulation packs:** invented neighborhoods and characters informed by the documented *type* of crisis, not a literal recreation of atrocities. Never use real survivor testimony without consent and rights clearance.
3. **Root-cause investigation:** distinguish documented acts, allegations, contested accounts, court findings, policy choices, conflict history, funding shortfalls, environmental shocks and institutional capacity. Avoid one-cause conspiracy narratives.
4. **Civilian systems builder:** water, sanitation, accessible shelters, clinics, education, logistics, solar power, food distribution, livelihoods and independent governance.
5. **Accountability and policy:** evidence literacy, public records, lawful advocacy, due process, fair procurement, humanitarian principles, and independent reporting.
6. **Real-world bridge:** optional links to independently verified relief organizations, volunteering and learning; no in-game money transfer, fake donation receipts, simulated "rescues" of real people or unverified local calls.

## Region packs and missions
Each pack uses a fictional location, original NPCs and explicit separation from the corresponding real-world source card.

### Sudan — Riverbend District (conflict, displacement, infrastructure)
- SD-01 **The Missing Supply Manifest:** local aid coordinator reconciles fictional incoming deliveries against needs, preventing duplicate counts.
- SD-02 **The Road Not Taken:** compare access constraints, lawful coordination and alternate civilian delivery methods; no armed checkpoints gameplay or covert routing.
- SD-03 **Water After the Outage:** build and maintain a conceptual water network, safe service queues and backup power.
- SD-04 **The Clinic Without Power:** prioritize fictional cold-chain and electricity continuity with qualified medical staff.
- SD-05 **The Return That Can Wait:** support voluntary, safe, dignified options and housing documentation without pressuring displaced people to return.
Evidence lead: 2026 Sudan humanitarian situation and conflict chronology (UK government September 2026; verify alongside OCHA/UNHCR before publication).

### Gaza / Occupied Palestinian Territory — Harbor of Hope (civilian protection, access, reconstruction)
- GZ-01 **What the Map Does Not Show:** distinguish destroyed infrastructure, reported needs and uncertain access from a fictional service map.
- GZ-02 **The Water Queue:** design equitable fictional WASH distribution, including accessibility and family needs.
- GZ-03 **The School in Two Shifts:** plan temporary learning spaces, safeguarding and teacher support.
- GZ-04 **A Hospital's Last Generator:** manage fictional fuel, backup power and clinical priorities with professional review, not treatment decisions.
- GZ-05 **Rebuild With Residents:** community-led reconstruction, documentation, land/tenure safeguards and independent oversight.
Evidence lead: UNFPA 2026 Humanitarian Action Overview and updated OCHA country materials. No reenactment of real attacks.

## "A Government Calls Itself Islamic" — accountability story mode
**Theme:** public claims of religious legitimacy do not exempt any government, military, corporation or community institution from evidence-based human-rights scrutiny. Avoid an automated declaration that an entire Muslim nation is "un-Islamic". Distinguish a state, its policies, the governing officials, residents, Muslim scholars, dissenters and minorities. Do not assert a disputed Quranic interpretation as settled law.

### Five playable fictional accountability missions
- AC-01 **The Official Speech:** compare a fictional minister's claim to publicly available policy text and dated rights reporting; mark what is verified, disputed or unreviewed.
- AC-02 **The Closed Door:** a fictional women's education initiative faces an official restriction. Hear affected students, teachers, parents, government representatives and qualified legal/scholarly reviewers; seek lawful, safe advocacy options.
- AC-03 **The Aid Agreement:** a fictional government announces humanitarian support, but players must distinguish pledge, committed funds, disbursement, delivered goods and actual civilian outcomes. No allegation of real state complicity without independently verified evidence.
- AC-04 **The Rights Hearing:** present a source-backed, nonviolent civil-rights petition through a fictional public process. No harassment, regime-change tactics, targeting, sabotage or instructions to evade authorities.
- AC-05 **The Charter That Outlives Power:** build independent audits, complaint mechanisms, inclusive service rules, anti-corruption controls and lawful governance.
**Optional scholarly panel:** explore source-backed Qur'anic themes of justice, trust, noncompulsion and consultation, but only after qualified review; no automated fatwa, takfir, religious purity scores or theological rulings on named contemporary leaders.

## Global campaign plot — The Empty Pledge
A fictional coalition promises a huge reconstruction fund. A glossy dashboard counts announcements as completed aid. A local coordinator notices that a water clinic has received nothing. The player must trace **pledged → committed → disbursed → delivered → operational** amounts, interview officials, verify documents and correct misinformation. Branches: work with reform-minded institutions, advocate publicly using verified records, or establish an independent civilian service partnership. Ending tests whether the system remains accountable when the protagonists leave.

## Source-led educational/LMS modes
Inspired by the *actual inspected* ummah.build weekly calendar: Islamic pain point -> builder spotlight -> thought leadership -> open-source resource -> community project -> optional real-world action. This is a learning **format**, not a copy of unpublished articles.
Inspired by the inspected *Perspectives of Muslim Founders* manuscript:
- **The Three-Asymmetry Test:** information, urgency and exit asymmetry for food, health, finance and humanitarian services.
- **Founder-to-Institution chain:** character -> product -> company -> institution -> civilization; audit unintended consequences at each scale.
- **Waqf continuity challenge:** corpus, beneficiaries, governance and maintenance; "who pays in year five?" with specialist legal/fiqh review.
- **Source discipline:** direct text, interpretation, applied ethics, product design and evidence are separate; the manuscript's six-level method must be checked against exact chapter before adapting detailed labels.

## Safety and quality gates
- Do not gamify deaths, famine, displacement, trauma or attacks; no "victim rescue score", battle reenactments, or exploitative imagery.
- Source cards carry as-of date, source, uncertainty, rights/license, geography and review state. Refresh on a schedule before publication; expired cards fail closed.
- Civilian needs never depend on faith; no forced dawah or religious identity collection in aid distribution.
- Survivor testimony and identifiable imagery require explicit consent, rights clearance and trauma review.
- No operational advice about checkpoints, military movements, sanctions evasion or evading surveillance.
- Real-world links to relief organizations must be verified and never fabricated. No real donations through the game without legal/payment safeguards.
- Scholar, regional human-rights, local humanitarian, safeguarding, legal and accessibility review before release.
- Provide skip, content warnings, calm alternative learning, reduced-motion and noncompetitive endings.
- Success measures are service continuity, accountability, informed consent, equitable access, local leadership and durable governance, not a player's moral worth.

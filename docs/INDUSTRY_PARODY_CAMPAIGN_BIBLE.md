# Dunya — Industry Parody Campaign Bible

**Draft fiction, not factual allegations.** This expands the existing eleven-chapter campaign without replacing it. Real brands below are reference points for recognizable product categories and publicly visible business models; **none is alleged to have done the fictional act**. Fictional corporations must use original names, logos, executives, products and assets. No disguised claim about a real company's criminality or coordination. Real case-study claims require independent primary sourcing, fair attribution and legal review.

## Core dramatic structure

The player first encounters a problem, interviews people with different interests, tests evidence, builds a sustainable alternative and submits it to independent audit. Rival employees have legitimate goals and can become allies. Outcomes measure fictional trust, access, service quality, fairness, reserves and resilience—not faith or divine rewards. All paths remain lawful and nonviolent.

**Five connected missions:** 1.1 **Audit the Feed** → 1.2 **Creator Walkout** → 1.3 **Quiet Settings** → 1.4 **Build a Timeline** → 1.5 **Creator Cooperative**.

**Three meaningful decisions:** choose between a fast commercial route and a slower transparent route; negotiate with an incumbent or build an independent service; prioritize immediate affordability or long-term sustainability. Specific chapter tension: ranking transparency, consent and creator livelihood.

**Player builds:** privacy-first platform, with operating expenses, staff, maintenance, accessible services, public feedback and governance. A premature shortcut causes a measurable defect; the player can disclose, repair, compensate fictional stakeholders when appropriate, and invite review.

## Cross-chapter threads

- **The Investor's Shortcut:** a recurring fictional investor offers useful capital with difficult governance conditions, not a secret conspiracy.
- **The Uncomfortable Ally:** an incumbent employee helps reform a flawed process without sabotaging their employer.
- **Bayhaven continuity:** the pantry, library and proposed mosque depend on media accuracy, accessible housing, transparent procurement and financial reserves.
- **The Year Without You:** a simulated year without protagonist control tests succession, maintenance, feedback and institutional independence.

## Production and review gates

Each mission needs unique mission_id, protagonist-specific scenes, named NPC motivations, location, evidence, triggers, prerequisites, three chapter-specific choices, alternate peaceful approaches, money/material/skill requirements, repair outcomes, persistent world changes, tests, accessibility, localization and source/legal/cultural review. The five titles above are story anchors, **not implemented mission definitions**. Do not invent real corporate scandals or represent satire as investigative fact. No real company is depicted as directed by Shaytan or any faith group.

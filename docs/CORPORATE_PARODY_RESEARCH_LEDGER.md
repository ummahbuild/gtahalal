## Research procedure
1. Start with official product documentation, filings and current policies; record date and jurisdiction.
2. Add regulator/court material only when directly relevant, with outcome and appeal status.
3. Include the company's explanation and competing expert analysis.
4. Identify the *general mechanism* for satire; invent a separate fictional incident with distinct characters.
5. Never imply a parody is evidence of a real scandal. Do not reproduce logos, slogans, trade dress or real employees.
6. Record reviewer, approval status, correction trigger and licensed assets before release.
